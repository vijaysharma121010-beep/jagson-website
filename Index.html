About Jagson

Years of Indian floors behind every robot we sell.

Jagson Robotics has spent years working for the Indian market, helping restaurants, hotels and factories save hours on jobs a machine can do. We have seen what a robot faces here: summer heat, uneven floors, power dips and packed dining rooms. We choose and set up every model with that in mind.

Each robot is tested in Mumbai, kept in stock in India and installed by our own engineers. Menus, languages and GST invoices are handled for you, and if something stops working, a Jagson engineer answers the phone.
